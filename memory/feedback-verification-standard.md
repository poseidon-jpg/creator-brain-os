Anything that matters is judged by a separate grader with a rubric (video-judge, critic), never by the agent that made it. Checks live out of reach of the change they judge. Prefer evidence the owner can see (a rendered video, frame grabs, a screenshot). A check still failing after three fix rounds stops the work and goes to the owner. Commits say how they were verified (Verified-by:).
