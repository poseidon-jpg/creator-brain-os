description: Finish every step of the agreed plan before adding new work
metadata:
  type: feedback
---

Never start side quests while steps of the plan the owner approved are still open. Close or explicitly block every original step first; new ideas go to the end of the queue unless they unblock an open step.

**Why:** long builds drift into shiny expansions while the core mission is half done.

**How to apply:** before any new step, check the plan for open items. "Partly done" is not done.
