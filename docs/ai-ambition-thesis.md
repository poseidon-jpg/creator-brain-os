# AI Ambition Thesis — the operating foundation

Source: RoboNuggets' guide to Felix Rieseberg's post on the three types of AI ambition (Oct 2026). This file is the working version, applied to a TikTok Shop affiliate business. Every task in this hub runs through it.

## The three directions

**Altitude (up): ask for the outcome, not the task.**
Before starting, name the goal beyond the goal: what should happen *next* because this work exists. Then let the approach follow from that, even if it means not producing the thing that was literally asked for.
- Best for business work with a measurable end state (sales, leads, conversions, a signed partner).
- Weaker for pure creative work. There, lean on Depth and Range instead.
- Set it up first: an outcome needs real data and a way to check it (analytics, ad dashboards, sales numbers).

**Depth (down): do it the way a top expert would. Use their methods, not just their vocabulary.**
Before starting, answer four questions:
1. What would a top professional do here that wasn't asked for?
2. What is the rawest material they would start from (raw data, transcripts, real ads, real customer words) instead of a summary?
3. Which specific methods and tools would they use?
4. What check, other than Claude or you, proves the result is right?
- Set ambitious targets and treat them as solvable. "Impossible" means keep going.
- **Outside judge, always, on work that matters.** Never let the model grade its own output. Use a tool, a metric, a test, or a separate reviewer subagent whose only job is to find what's wrong.

**Range (sideways): bring in other fields.**
- Swap the noun for verbs. Not "a VSL" but "make a skeptical stranger believe, want, and act in 12 minutes". Then ask which fields do each verb best (direct-response copywriter, behavioral psychologist, film editor, sound designer).
- Assemble a crew for important work: about five experts from different fields, two of them adversarial (red team). Use their *methods*, never role-play their voices.
- Crews cost tokens. Save them for high-stakes work.

## Applied to this hub

| Area | Default direction | Outside judge |
|---|---|---|
| Shop & Products (picking products, samples, commissions) | Altitude first, then Depth | Real numbers: GMV, commission earned, conversion rate, sample-to-post rate |
| Video Studio (editing, captions, variants) | Depth + Range | The `video-judge` agent with its rubric, then real retention and GMV per video |
| Creative Lab (hooks, angles, scripts) | Depth + Range | Separate critic subagent; what actually sold in `analytics/` |
| Building this hub / systems | Altitude | Smoke tests (`brain.js recall` finds the fact), the map shows reality |

## Why it needs a why

Ambition without a reason just means going faster. Keep the why visible. Pace it, ship the ambitious version, improve each round, and pause when needed.
