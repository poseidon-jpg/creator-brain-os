# Hook bank

Hooks are grouped by **shape** (the move it makes), not by wording. Rotate shapes; don't rank them until your own numbers exist (see `analytics/learnings.md`). Every hook must show the product or its result inside the first 3 seconds. *(TikTok Shop Academy structure: hook → key message → CTA, hook in the first 3-6s, tied to the product. VERIFIED via secondary write-up; see research/lane-a-creative-meta.md)*

| Shape | The move | Example skeleton |
|---|---|---|
| **Price / deal** | Lead with the price or the deal | "$12 and it replaced my $80 one." |
| Result-first | Show the end state before anything else | "This is what my [thing] looks like after one use." |
| Problem-first | Name the pain they have right now | "If your [problem] keeps happening, watch this." |
| Specific number | A concrete number makes it real | "I tried 4 of these. Only one was worth $12." |
| Contrarian | Push against what they believe | "Stop buying [category] at [store]." |
| Comparison | Versus beats description | "$15 vs $90, and I can't tell the difference." |
| Confession | A small honest admission | "I didn't think this would work. I was wrong." |
| Sound-first | Let the product's own sound open it | (the click, pour, crunch, snap, no words for 1s) |
| Reply-to-comment | Answer a real comment on screen | "Replying to @…: yes it actually fits in a carry-on." |

## Measured priors (start here, then let your own sales overrule)
Sales per view vs the average top seller, MediaLabs (TikTok Shop agency), n=9,285 analysed of 51,843 tracked, 19 Sep-18 Oct 2026. These are associations, not causes, and the source sells services (M3, research/corpus-measured.md):
| Hook shape | Sales per view |
|---|---|
| Price / deal | **1.53x** |
| Bold claim (that you can back up) | 1.28x |
| Face in the first second | 1.05x |
| Demo-first | 0.89x |
So for each batch, make at least one price/deal variant, and don't default to demo-first openers.

## Three layers in every opening *(SAID, multiple operators)*
1. What you **say** (from your raw audio)
2. The **on-screen headline text** in the first 3 seconds. It teases and never gives the whole payoff.
3. The **visual**: the product moving, or its result
