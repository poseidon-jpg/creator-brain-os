02 / Governance

Most finance AI fails at the same fence: it produces a number nobody can stand behind. A forecast with no lineage. A journal with no reviewer. An agent with admin rights and no log.

We build the opposite way. Every figure a LEDGERLINE agent touches carries its evidence with it, so when the auditor, the board, or the bank asks "where did this come from," the answer is one click, not one week.

  • G.01Immutable action logEvery read, calculation, and posting recorded to an append-only store. Nothing is edited in place, ever.
  • G.02Human gates on materialityAgents propose; named approvers post. Thresholds set with your auditors, enforced in code.
  • G.03Source-linked figuresEach number resolves to its ERP transactions, bank lines, or model assumptions. Lineage is a feature, not a report.
  • G.04Segregation of duties, mappedAgent permissions mirror your SoD matrix. An agent that codes invoices cannot approve them.

05 / FAQ
Will our auditors accept AI-prepared work?+

Yes, when the controls are designed for it. Agents prepare; named humans review and approve above materiality thresholds. Three clients have now been through big-four year-end audits with LEDGERLINE agents in scope, with zero findings against agent-performed work. We involve your audit partner during design, not after go-live.

Does the AI post journal entries on its own?+

Not above your thresholds. Agents draft entries with full source citations and queue them for a named approver. Low-risk, high-volume items like standard recurring journals can auto-post if you choose, and every posting is logged either way. You set the line; the system enforces it.

What happens to our data? Is it used to train models?+

Your financial data stays in your environment or a dedicated tenant, encrypted in transit and at rest. We use commercial API agreements with zero training retention, and we will put that in writing in the MSA. Nothing you close with us trains anyone's model.

How fast do we see payback?+

The first agent typically pays for itself within one to two quarters through recovered hours, kept discounts, and reduced audit prep. The assessment gives you the payback math for your volumes before you commit to a build.

We are mid-market, not enterprise. Is this for us?+

Mid-market is our center of gravity: finance teams of 3 to 30 running NetSuite, Xero, or QuickBooks. Big enough that the close hurts, small enough that a controller still touches everything. That is exactly where agents return the most hours.
