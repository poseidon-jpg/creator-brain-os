Tone & brand safety

It speaks for your property. So we treat that as sacred.

An AI that greets your guests is wearing your uniform. Before anything goes live, we build guardrails a GM would sign off on, because one does.

Your voice, codified

We interview your GM and best front-desk staff, then distill a written voice guide: greetings, sign-offs, how you say no, when you offer a gesture. The agent is tested against it line by line before launch.

Escalation rules, in writing

Hard lines the agent never crosses without a human:

  • Complaints involving safety, health, or billing disputes
  • Compensation beyond your pre-approved gesture menu
  • Media, legal, or anything that smells like either
  • A guest who asks for a person, immediately and graciously

Human handoff that feels seamless

When a thread escalates, your team receives the full conversation, guest profile, and a one-line summary. The guest is told a named colleague is picking up, never dropped into a queue. Average handoff context read: 20 seconds.

Questions, answered

The things every GM asks on the first call.

Will guests know they are talking to an AI? +

If you want them to, yes, and we recommend it. The agent introduces itself as your digital concierge and never pretends to be a person. Guests care about a fast, warm, correct answer far more than who typed it. Disclosure language is yours to approve.

What happens when it does not know the answer? +

It says so gracefully and hands off. The agent only answers from your verified property knowledge and live PMS data. Anything outside that, or anything touching your escalation rules, goes to a named staff member with the full thread attached.

Does it work with our PMS and channels? +

We integrate with Mews, Cloudbeds, Opera, and apaleo out of the box, plus WhatsApp, SMS, email, and your booking engine chat. If you run something rarer, the audit week is where we confirm the connection before you commit.

How is guest data handled? +

Guest data stays inside your region, is never used to train public models, and is processed under a DPA we sign with every property. Access is logged, retention follows your policy, and you can export or delete everything at any time.

What does it cost? +

A flat monthly fee per property based on key count and modules, starting at $890/month for guest messaging on an independent boutique. No per-message charges, no revenue share. The audit gives you a precise quote before any commitment.
