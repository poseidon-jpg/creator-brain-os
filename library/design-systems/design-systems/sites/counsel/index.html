Questions of First Impression

What managing partners ask us.

Does client data ever touch a public model?+

No. Engagements run either fully on-premises or in a dedicated private tenancy with zero-retention agreements executed with every model provider. Nothing your firm processes is used to train anyone's models, ours included, and we will put that in the engagement letter.

How do you handle privilege?+

Privilege screens run before any document reaches a model: pattern detection, custodian rules, and your privilege log conventions. Flagged material is quarantined for human review. In four years and 2.4 million documents, we have had zero privilege incidents.

Will this survive a defensibility challenge in discovery?+

Our TAR workflows follow established case law on technology-assisted review, and every project ships with statistically validated recall reporting, seed-set documentation, and an expert declaration template. We have supported meet-and-confers and have yet to have a protocol rejected.

What does an engagement cost?+

The Privileged Audit is a fixed fee. Pilots and deployments are scoped per matter and typically land between the cost of one and three associate-years, with documented returns several multiples of that in year one. We will give you the number before you commit to anything.

Our insurers and clients have AI restrictions. Can you work within them?+

Yes, that is half our governance practice. We map every deployment against your professional indemnity terms and your clients' outside counsel guidelines, and we draft the disclosures and questionnaire responses for you.
