Deployment & safety model

Your data never becomes our data.

Most AI vendors ask hospitals to trust a black box in someone else's cloud. We invert that: the models come to your data, every inference is logged, and your compliance team gets the paperwork before your clinicians get the software.

  • PHI never leaves your VPC

    Models deploy inside your private cloud or on-prem hardware. No PHI in our systems, no PHI in model training, ever.

  • Every inference is auditable

    Full audit logs: input hash, model version, output, and the clinician who reviewed it. Your compliance team can replay any decision.

  • Clinician-in-the-loop by design

    No model output reaches a chart or a worklist without a licensed clinician's review. AI drafts and flags; your people decide.

  • Kill switch and rollback, tested

    Every deployment ships with a one-click disable path and a rehearsed rollback plan. We test it with your IT team before go-live.

FAQ

The questions your compliance team will ask first.

If you have one we have not covered, bring it to the readiness review. We would rather answer it before the contract than after.

Does any patient data ever reach Pulseframe's systems?
No. Models run inside your VPC or on your hardware. Our engineers work through your access controls, on your infrastructure, under your BAA. The only data that leaves is de-identified performance metrics, and only with your sign-off.
Are your models trained on our patients' data?
Base models are trained on licensed and public datasets. If an engagement calls for fine-tuning on your data, that training happens inside your environment, the resulting weights belong to you, and nothing is pooled across clients.
How does clinical validation actually work?
Before go-live, the model runs in a silent trial: it produces output on real cases but clinicians never see it in their workflow. A review panel of your physicians grades that output against ground truth. You set the acceptance threshold; we do not ship until the model clears it.
What happens if a model starts drifting after deployment?
Every deployment includes drift monitoring with alert thresholds agreed at go-live. If performance moves outside the band, the system alerts your team and ours, and the one-click disable path takes the model out of the workflow while we investigate. Quarterly revalidation is standard.
Do you integrate with Epic, Cerner, and our PACS?
Yes. We integrate through HL7 FHIR, native EHR APIs, and DICOM for imaging. Clinicians work in the tools they already use; there is no separate portal to log into.
What does an engagement cost?
The readiness review is a fixed fee. Build and deployment phases are scoped from its findings, so you see the full cost before committing to anything beyond the review. Most systems fund the first deployment from a single line item: recovered clinician hours.
