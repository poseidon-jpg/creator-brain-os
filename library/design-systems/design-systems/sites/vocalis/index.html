FAQ

The questions every ops lead asks us

Will callers know they are talking to an AI?
Yes, by design. The agent discloses it is an automated assistant at the start of the call, as required in most jurisdictions. Disclosure costs nothing: our post-disclosure CSAT averages 94% because the agent answers instantly and actually resolves things.
What happens when the agent gets stuck?
It hands off. Every deployment ships with escalation rules: to your team during hours, to a callback queue or answering partner after hours. The human receives the full transcript and a one-line summary, so the caller never repeats themselves.
Is voice cloning legal and safe?
We only clone voices with written, revocable consent from the speaker, stored in a consent vault. Cloned voices are watermarked, locked to your telephony, and never reusable outside your account. If consent is revoked, the model is deleted within 72 hours.
How do you handle our industry's compliance rules?
Guardrails are configured per industry: HIPAA-conscious flows for health, no-advice boundaries for insurance and finance, PCI-compliant payment capture via tokenized IVR handoff. Recordings and transcripts live in your region, with retention you control.
What does an engagement cost?
Setup runs $8k to $25k depending on integrations, then a monthly managed fee tied to call volume. Most clients see the system pay for itself within the first quarter from recovered missed-call revenue alone. The voice audit gives you the exact math before you commit.
