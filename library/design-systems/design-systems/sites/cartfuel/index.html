Straight answers

Things founders ask us first.

How fast do we see revenue impact?+
The pilot system goes live on production traffic within 5 weeks. Most clients see a measurable lift against holdout within the first 30 days after launch. If the audit says your store will not pay back in a quarter, we tell you and part friends.
Will an AI agent embarrass our brand in support?+
It launches in shadow mode first: drafting replies your team approves. Only once approval rates pass 95% do we let it resolve autonomously, and it always escalates refund exceptions, legal topics and angry customers to a human with full context attached.
Do you replace our dev or marketing team?+
No. We ship into your existing stack and hand over documented, monitored systems. Your team keeps ownership; we stay on as the AI growth layer - building, testing and tuning what in-house teams rarely have bandwidth for.
What does an engagement cost?+
Pilots start at $12K for a single system with a defined KPI. Ongoing growth retainers run $8-25K/month depending on scope. Every invoice sits next to an attribution dashboard, so ROI is never a debate.
Who owns the models and the data?+
You do. Everything trains on your first-party data inside your cloud accounts. If we ever part ways, the systems, prompts, pipelines and dashboards stay with you, fully documented.
