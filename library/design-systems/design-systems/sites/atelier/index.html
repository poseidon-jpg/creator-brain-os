Our AI stance

How we use AI, honestly

Always human

  • Strategy, positioning, and the founding idea
  • Logo marks, wordmarks, and type selection
  • Art direction and every final approval
  • The conversation with you

Gladly machine

  • Resizing one campaign into 200 placements
  • Localising copy and layouts across markets
  • First-pass moodboards and reference sweeps
  • QA passes on colour, contrast, and spec

Our rule is simple: AI never makes a creative decision, and a human never does robot work. We publish which tools touched every deliverable, we never train models on one client's work for another, and anything a machine generates is reviewed by a named art director before you see it.

Process

Four weeks to a system,
then it compounds

A fixed cadence with two standing reviews a week. You always know what exists and what ships next.

01

Listen

Week one

Stakeholder interviews, audit of everything you have shipped, and a positioning workshop. We leave with the sentence your brand has to prove.

02

Shape

Weeks two to three

Senior designers explore by hand: marks, type pairings, motion behaviours. Machines run reference sweeps in the background; humans decide what survives.

03

Encode

Week four

The chosen system is written into tokens, templates, and a fine-tuned generation pipeline with your rules baked in. This is where most studios hand over a PDF; we hand over an engine.

04

Produce

Ongoing

Monthly production retainers. You brief, the pipeline drafts, our art directors gate, you publish. Volume scales without new headcount on either side.

The studio

Atelier Nine started in 2021 when two art directors and a machine-learning engineer noticed the same thing: 70 percent of studio hours were spent resizing, not designing. We rebuilt the studio so those hours go to machines and the rest goes to craft.
