The valuation model

Built on what the market actually did, not what it hopes.

Most "estimates" are a median with a marketing budget. Ours is a market-specific model, retrained monthly, that shows its working: every range ships with the sales that justify it.

  • 01
    Settled sales, 14 years deep

    State valuer-general records and portal history, cleaned and deduplicated per suburb.

  • 02
    Live listing dynamics

    Asking-price movement, time on market, and withdrawal signals from current campaigns.

  • 03
    Property attributes, 40+ signals

    Land size, aspect, renovation state from imagery, school catchments, flood overlays, walkability.

  • 04
    Your agency's own outcomes

    Appraisal-to-sale gaps from your book feed back in, so the model learns your patch best.

Accuracy varies by suburb and stock depth. We publish per-market error rates to every client and flag low-confidence estimates rather than bluffing them.
