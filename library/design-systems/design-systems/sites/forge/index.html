SEC 05

Questions plant teams actually ask.

FAQ REV A
Q-01Does our data leave the plant?+

Not unless you want it to. The default architecture keeps training data and inference on-site on edge hardware. Cloud is opt-in for fleet dashboards only, and fully air-gapped deployments are available for regulated sites.

Q-02We run 20-year-old PLCs. Is that a problem?+

No. Roughly half our installs sit next to legacy Siemens S7 or Allen-Bradley SLC hardware. We read via OPC UA, Modbus or a protocol gateway, and we default to read-only access so your control logic is never touched.

Q-03How much historical data do we need?+

Less than you think. Vision QC needs sample parts, not history, as we generate training data during install. Predictive maintenance works best with 6+ months of sensor data, but we can install sensors and start the clock during the pilot.

Q-04What does an engagement cost?+

The feasibility sprint is a fixed fee of $18K. Pilots typically run $60K to $140K including hardware, depending on line count and camera stations. Every proposal states the payback math up front, and we walk away from projects that do not clear it.

Q-05Who maintains the models after handover?+

Your team runs day-to-day operations after training. We monitor model health remotely (or by signed package on air-gapped sites) and retrain when drift appears, under a support agreement sized to your fleet.
