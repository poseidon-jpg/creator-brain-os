Common questions

Before you book the briefing.

Do you replace our existing SOC or security team?
No. We layer detection models and our operations grid on top of your current stack and team. Your analysts keep ownership; they just stop doing tier-1 triage by hand. Teams with no SOC at all can run fully managed on our grid.
We only have one customer-facing LLM feature. Is a red-team audit overkill?
One feature is exactly when it is cheapest to fix. A scoped audit on a single assistant runs two to three weeks, and the findings usually harden the patterns your team reuses for everything you ship after it.
How does pricing work?
Audits and supply-chain hardening are fixed-fee projects, quoted after the baseline week. AI-SOC monitoring and compliance automation are monthly retainers sized by telemetry volume and control scope. No per-alert pricing, ever; it rewards the wrong behavior.
Where does our data live?
Telemetry stays in your tenant; our detection layer deploys into your cloud accounts and we operate it remotely. For red-team engagements, transcripts and findings live in a dedicated encrypted enclave per client and are destroyed on your schedule.
Can you work under our existing SOC 2 or ISO scope?
Yes. We are SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 certified ourselves, and we routinely operate as a documented subprocessor inside client scopes. Our compliance team hands your auditor the vendor package before they ask.
