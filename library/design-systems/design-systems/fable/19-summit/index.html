Gear Philosophy

Carry less.
Trust more.

Above 8,000 metres every gram is a decision. We issue, test and retire gear on a fixed cycle - nothing on your harness is older than two seasons.

01

Oxygen: unlimited, not rationed

Four litres per minute available on summit day. Most operators budget three bottles per climber. We cache five, plus reserves at the Balcony and South Summit.

02

Double 8mm fixed lines

Our teams fix independent up and down lines on crux sections, so a queue on the Hillary Step never becomes a countdown on your oxygen.

03

-40°C rated, personally fitted

Down suits, boots and mitts fitted at our Kathmandu depot two weeks before the walk-in. Cold injuries are a fitting problem before they are a weather problem.

04

Radios that survive summit day

Every climber carries a personal VHF plus satellite tracker. Base Camp knows your altitude, heart rate and position every sixty seconds from tent to summit.
