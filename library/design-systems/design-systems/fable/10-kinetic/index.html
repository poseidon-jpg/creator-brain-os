gravity

Study 03 · Cascade / Vantage
CASCADE

Per-glyph choreography. Each letter enters on its own curve, offset by 60 milliseconds of scroll distance. Type as a wave, not a block.

05 / The foundry

Kinetic was founded on one stubborn belief: a letterform is not a picture, it is a behaviour.

Founded by Mara Ilves and Deon Okafor in a former printing house on the Maashaven, Kinetic drew masters for interpolation before drawing single weights. Every family is designed axis-first: we sketch the extremes, then engineer the journey between them. If a face can't survive being animated, it doesn't ship.
