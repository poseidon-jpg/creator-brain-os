The label

VELVET began in 2019 as a lathe-cut experiment in a Rotterdam basement: twenty copies, hand-numbered, gone in a night. We never stopped cutting.

We release eight records a year, never more. Each one is mastered specifically for vinyl by Anneke Vos at Duplex Mastering, pressed at 180 grams, and sleeved in artwork generated from the audio itself — the grooves you hear are the geometry you see.

No playlists pitched, no trends chased. If a record still gives us chills on the fortieth listen, it comes out. If it doesn’t, it doesn’t. That is the entire A&R strategy, and it has never failed us.
