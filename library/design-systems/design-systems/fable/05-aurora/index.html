02 - Research Programs

Four programs, one changing Arctic.

Funded through 2031 by the Nordheim Polar Trust and a consortium of nine universities.

AL-01 · SINCE 2019

Magnetosphere–Ionosphere Coupling

All-sky cameras, a 64-channel riometer and our fluxgate magnetometer array track how solar wind energy cascades into the upper atmosphere - the same energy that paints the sky above the station nearly every clear night.

ALL-SKY IMAGINGRIOMETRYSUBSTORMS
AL-02 · SINCE 2021

Ice Core Paleoclimate

The Vestfonna drilling line has recovered 612 metres of ice - a 12,000-year archive. Isotope ratios and trapped air bubbles let us read past summers the way tree rings read forests.

AL-03 · SINCE 2022

Permafrost Carbon Flux

Eleven boreholes and an eddy-covariance tower measure how much ancient carbon the thawing ground exhales. Borehole BH-7 has warmed 1.4 °C at two metres depth since installation.

EDDY COVARIANCECH₄ / CO₂11 BOREHOLES
AL-04 · SINCE 2024

Polar Night Ecology

Life doesn't stop when the sun sets for four months. Acoustic moorings in Kongsfjorden and low-light drone surveys map an ecosystem that runs on moonlight, starlight - and aurora.

04 - Expedition Log

Field notes from the edge of the map.

2026-07-02 · 03:14 UTC

Midnight sun flux campaign, day 9

Nine straight days of 24-hour daylight and the flux tower hasn't blinked. CO₂ uptake over the tundra peaked at 14:00 solar time - the strongest midsummer drawdown we've logged at Site B. Reindeer chewed through a guy-line flag again. The flag lost.

— S. Lehtinen, AL-03
2026-06-18 · 22:47 UTC

BH-7 hits a record

Borehole BH-7 read −2.08 °C at two metres - the warmest June figure in our series and 0.3 °C above 2025. We re-ran the calibration twice hoping for an instrument fault. It isn't one.

— I. Halvorsen, Station Leader
2026-04-11 · 01:52 UTC

The G3 storm

Kp 7 for six hours. Corona overhead, red lower borders, the whole fjord lit green enough to read by. The all-sky camera captured 14,000 frames; Kenji hasn't slept and refuses to. Magnetometer swung −612 nT at peak.

— K. Okabe, AL-01
2026-02-27 · 11:05 UTC

First light

The sun cleared Zeppelinfjellet for four minutes today - first direct light on station since November 3rd. Tradition held: everyone outside, faces south, pancakes after. Vestfonna core segment 588 m arrived intact by snowcat.

— J. Møller, Station Chef
2026-01-09 · 14:30 UTC

Acoustic mooring K-2 recovered

Four months of polar-night audio from 40 m depth in Kongsfjorden. First pass: cod choruses under total darkness and a bearded seal that sings, apparently, only during aurora. Emil insists this is coincidence. Emil is checking anyway.

— E. Nordvik, AL-04

Applications for the 2027 overwintering crew open 1 September 2026. Two research fellowships, one engineering post, and yes - the chef position is contested.
