Fold Library

Six folds, a lifetime of paper.

Every model in our library starts with a 15 cm square. Difficulty is rated in mountains - one for a first evening, five for a month of quiet frustration and joy.

Classic Crane

Orizuru · the first fold

The one everyone begins with. Petal folds, a bird base, and the moment the wings pull apart and the body inflates with a single breath.

22 steps · 40 min

Waterbomb

Fūsen · the balloon

A sealed cube you inflate at the last step. Our favourite first lesson in 3D thinking - the fold that teaches paper to hold air.

14 steps · 20 min

Jumping Frog

Kaeru · the party trick

Press the back crease and it leaps. Built on the frog base, it hides more engineering than anything else in a beginner class.

18 steps · 30 min

Kawasaki Rose

Bara · the spiral

A twist-fold rose that curls into itself like the real flower. The collapse at step 31 is the closest paper gets to magic.

47 steps · 2.5 hrs

Flapping Bird

Habataku tori · the toy

Pull the tail and the wings beat. A crane with a mechanism inside - the fold we use to explain tension and stored energy.

26 steps · 50 min

Ryūjin Dragon

Ryūjin 3.5 · the summit

Satoshi Kamiya's sea dragon: 275 steps, thousands of scales, one uncut square. Nobody finishes it fast. Some never finish it at all.

275 steps · ~1 month

Commissions

Paper, at any scale.

We build origami for windows, weddings, film sets, and lobbies - from a single two-metre crane to ten thousand suspended butterflies. Everything folded by hand in the studio, installed anywhere in the world.

Recent work: the 3,000-crane ceiling at Hotel Kanade Ginza, the folding title sequence for the film Paper Moon Over Osaka, and a wind-responsive facade study with Studio Arata Architects.
