SECTION 02 · METHOD

Drawn first. Built to last.

Three rules govern every project that leaves this office. They have not changed since our first sketch in 2009, and we do not expect them to.

RULE 01

The hand before the machine

Every project starts as graphite on 90gsm trace. If a scheme cannot survive being drawn at 1:500 with a clutch pencil, it does not deserve a render. Software joins on week three, never week one.

RULE 02

Structure is the ornament

We do not decorate. The rhythm of columns, the depth of a beam, the shadow of a reveal - that is the entire aesthetic budget. If a detail cannot explain how the building stands up, it is deleted.

RULE 03

The hundred-year view

Materials are chosen for how they weather, not how they photograph. Zinc, rammed earth, blackbutt, lime render. We design the building at year one and check it again, on paper, at year one hundred.

NO. 0117JINDABYNE NSWCOMPLETED 2024

Meridian House

A 340 m² alpine residence held one metre above the frost line on twin steel blades. The living hall runs the full thirty-metre length under a single folded zinc roof, so that winter sun reaches the southern wall at 10:14 each morning - we drew the ray before we drew the room.

AREA 340 m²  ·  STRUCTURE steel blade + CLT
ENVELOPE zinc, rammed earth  ·  SHEETS ISSUED 74
ELEVATION / PLAN

NO. 0139CASTLEMAINE VICCOMPLETED 2023

The Kiln

A 1,850 m² ceramics centre for the Loddon Arts Trust. Five sawtooth bays throw soft southern light across forty potters' wheels, while a rebuilt anagama kiln anchors the east end - the chimney is the tallest brick structure raised in the shire since 1911.

AREA 1 850 m²  ·  STRUCTURE recycled brick + glulam
CLIENT Loddon Arts Trust  ·  SHEETS ISSUED 128
ELEVATION / PLAN

NO. 0152BRISBANE QLDON SITE · DUE 2027

Fold Tower

An 88-metre mixed-use tower whose plan rotates through a single six-degree fold as it rises, shading itself against the subtropical west. Twenty-two plates, one geometry, zero curtain-wall extrusions repeated from a catalogue. Currently at level 9 of 22.

HEIGHT 88 m · 22 plates  ·  STRUCTURE concrete core + steel fold
PROGRAM workspace / 41 apartments  ·  SHEETS ISSUED 412 (and counting)
