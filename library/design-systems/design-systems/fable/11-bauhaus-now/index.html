01 / Manifesto

In December 1926 the Dessau building opened its glass curtain wall to the world. One hundred years later, we reopen it as a question: what would the Bauhaus build now?

BAUHAUS NOW is sixteen days of exhibitions, workshops and stage experiments inside the building Walter Gropius designed and the city that grew around it. It is not a museum show. Every room is a working room. You will leave with ink on your hands.

We invited 74 designers, weavers, typographers, roboticists and choreographers from 23 countries to answer the school's original brief with today's materials: recycled aluminium, mycelium board, code, and light. The results hang, spin, and perform across nine halls.

Prof. Katrin Oswalt, Festival Director · Anni-Albers-Halle, Dessau

04 / Play

The Vorkurs began with three shapes and three colours. So does this. Click the canvas to drop shapes; every composition is a legal Bauhaus poster. Probably.

Press Shuffle for a machine-made composition in the spirit of Bayer's 1923 exhibition posters, or clear the sheet and build your own. Your poster stays on the sheet until you leave - like all good student work, it is gloriously temporary.

Getting here

Direct trains from Berlin Hbf every hour (1 h 40). From Dessau Hauptbahnhof it is a 12-minute walk down Bauhausstraße - follow the red dashes painted on the pavement for the festival. Cycle parking under the workshop wing; the building has step-free access at the north entrance.

Open hours

Exhibitions daily 10:00–20:00 · Werkhalle Nord until 22:00 · rooftop projections from 21:30, weather willing. The canteen serves the 1926 menu (updated: the semolina is now optional) from 09:00.
