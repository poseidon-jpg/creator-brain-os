03.The interview

"They built a $2 million park and everyone still skates the gas station. That should tell you everything."

Moss Delgado, 31, has been the loudest thing in this city's skate scene for a decade - frontside grinds you can hear from two blocks, opinions you can hear from three. In March he quit his logistics job, sold his car, and started skating in $12 court shoes "out of spite."

We got two hours in the Level 4 parking garage before security got curious. Extracts below. Full thing on p.24, unedited, swearing intact.

GRIND: You keep every broken deck. Why?

"They're receipts. Proof I was there. My landlord counts them when he's deciding whether to evict me."

GRIND: What's wrong with the new park?

"Everything is a perfect radius. Nothing in a real city is a perfect radius. They built a skatepark for a video game."
