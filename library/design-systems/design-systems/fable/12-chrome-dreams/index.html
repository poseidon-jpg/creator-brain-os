Manifesto

The year 2000 promised us flying interfaces. We're here to deliver, 26 years late.

Somewhere between the millennium bug and the flat-design decade, the web lost its shine. Chrome Dreams is a working group of designers, 3D artists, and engineers who believe the future should look like it did on the cover of a 1999 tech magazine: silver, curved, glowing, and unreasonably hopeful.

01

Shine is sincerity

A reflective surface hides nothing. We design interfaces that gleam because they have nothing to be ashamed of - no dark patterns, no fine print, no grey areas. If it can't be chromed, it can't ship.

02

Optimism is a technology

Y2K design believed technology would make life better, and said so in gradients. We build with that same conviction: every project must make at least one person feel like the future is worth showing up for.

03

Curves over corners

Nature has no right angles and neither did the iMac G3. Our layouts breathe, our buttons are pills, and our type flows like mercury. Sharp edges are reserved for our opinions.

04

The blob is alive

Every Chrome Dreams project contains at least one living element - something that morphs, reacts, and refuses to sit still. Static design is a rehearsal. We only do opening nights.

Members

Six people, four time zones, one shared hallucination.

We met in the comment section of a WinAmp skin archive in 2023. Now we take on three client projects a year and spend the rest of the time building things nobody asked for.

Selected Projects

Work that reflects well on everyone involved.

2026

MERCURY OS

A concept operating system where every window is a droplet. Built for the Berlin Digital Futures biennale; 40,000 visitors dragged liquid windows around a 6-metre touchscreen.

Exhibition 2025

SOFT SERVE RECORDS

Full identity for an ambient label: chrome-dipped wordmark, iridescent vinyl sleeves, and a release site where each album is a playable metallic blob.

Identity + Web 2025

AIRPORT 2000

Wayfinding concept for Ōita Prefecture's regional terminal: pill-shaped signage, holographic gradients that shift with the time of day, zero right angles.

Environmental 2024

DIAL-UP DREAMHOUSE

Our founding project. A browser museum of Y2K web artifacts - 56k modem sounds included - visited by 1.2 million nostalgic humans in its first month.
