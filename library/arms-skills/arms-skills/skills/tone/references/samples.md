# Samples - your real voice

Your real writing and speech, so the agent copies how you actually sound instead of guessing. Read the samples that match the channel before drafting.

Keep it lean. About 3 samples per channel is enough. When a better one comes in, swap out the weakest. Never paste whole long documents; pick the paragraph that sounds most like you.

## How to add a sample

Copy this block, one per sample:

```
### <channel> - <short label>

> <the text, word for word>

Why it works: <one line on what to copy from it, such as the opener, the sentence length, a phrase you use>
```

Channels: spoken (video or voice), chat, post, newsletter, email, DM, short-form script, or any channel you use.
