# Sample batch: three quick reels

Read these top to bottom in one take. Pause for a breath between reels, then cut them apart later.

---

## Reel 1 - The two-minute rule

If a task takes less than two minutes, just do it now.

Reply to that message, file that receipt, book that appointment.

Because the time it takes to write it down and come back to it later is usually longer than the task itself.

So next time something small lands on your plate, ask yourself one question: is this under two minutes? If it is, it's done before it ever hits your list.

---

## Reel 2 - Batch your email

Checking email all day feels productive, but it breaks your focus every single time.

What works better is picking two set times, say eleven in the morning and four in the afternoon, and answering everything in one sitting.

Close the inbox the rest of the day.

You'll answer just as fast as before, and you get back long stretches where you can actually think.

---

## Reel 3 - Plan tomorrow tonight

The best time to plan your day is the night before.

Before you shut the laptop, write down the three things that would make tomorrow a good day, in order.

That's it, three things.

So when you sit down in the morning, you skip the "where do I start" part and go straight into the first one.
