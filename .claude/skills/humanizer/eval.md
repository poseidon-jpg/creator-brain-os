# Humanizer self-check (eval)

Run this on your OWN rewrite before you show it. Answer each line pass or fail. Any fail means fix the draft and run the checks again.

For a Detect request, skip the rewrite checks. Just confirm: every pattern named, every line quoted, a one-line fix each, no rewrite, no score, no AI-or-not verdict, and a closing offer to edit.

## Voice preserved (minimum effective edit)

1. Would the writer recognize this as their own voice?
2. Did I keep the distinctive vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor, uncertainty, and digressions?
3. Did I leave strong human sentences alone instead of tidying them for consistency?
4. Is the amount cut proportional to the actual slop, with no over-compression that strips character?
5. Did I keep "I think / maybe / honestly / to be real" where they carry real uncertainty or spoken rhythm (and cut them only where empty)?

## Slop removed

6. Are the 🔴 dead giveaways gone: candor markers, fake-context openers, faux-insight setups, significance puffery, chatbot artifacts, emojis, fake-profound kickers?
7. Are the 🟠 strong tells gone: conjunctive-adverb chains, colon-list intros, colon reveals, copula avoidance, weasel attribution, negative parallelism, dramatic fragmentation, rhetorical bait?
8. Are weak verb phrases replaced with direct verbs, and "is / are / has" used where clearer?
9. Are vague claims replaced with names, numbers, dates, or mechanisms, or flagged for the writer when there's no source, never invented?
10. Does the ending land on a concrete point, takeaway, or next action, with no summary-recap and no generic-positive close?

## Hard writing rules

11. ZERO em dashes. Not one. Treat this as a hard rule, and let it override the softer "1-2 are fine" advice you'll see in other slop guides. (If your own house style allows a couple, relax this line - but zero is the safest default.)
12. Straight quotes, not curly. Sentence case in headings. No decorative bold, no emoji.
13. Right context mode applied: script = written for the ear, social = one idea and no preamble, newsletter = personal, article = balanced?

## Final read

14. Read it aloud. Does it sound like a person talking, not a term paper?
15. Does sentence shape vary for real reasons, not robotic symmetry or stacked punchy fragments?
16. Was this checked directly against this file, not by spinning up a separate evaluator?
17. Does the output include the full rewrite plus a short **What changed** section?
